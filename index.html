HOW TO PLAY

Three moves. Zero shame.

  1. AIM

    Drag a thumb like a trackpad. The reticle follows along the street below.

  2. HOLD

    Hock it up. The longer you hold, the further it flies: pavement, road, park.

  3. RELEASE

    Let it fly and lead the walkers. Reach the level's target or it's over.

FEATURES

Every spit counts.

WANTED bounties

Now and then someone on the street gets marked. Soak them before they get away and the bounty pays double.

Combos and green spit

Hit six in a row and your points double. Earn a green spit and it splashes wide.

A new face every level

Each level has a target score and one newcomer. Miss the target and it's over: no lives, no retries.

The Daily Poost

Your best hit of the game makes the front page, headline and all.

Executive Orders

Three standing orders carry over from game to game. Sign them off for XP and climb from Intern to Emperor of the Rooftop.

Thirty-nine trophies

Joke-named and counted for life: COVFEFE, BIGLY, SAD!, STABLE GENIUS and the rest.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Where can I play it?

On iPhone and in the browser. Both are coming soon.

When does it come out?

Soon. It's in play-testing right now, one spit at a time.

Is that really his voice?

No. He's voiced by an impressionist who agreed to it. No real politician's voice was cloned.

Who's on the street?

Archetypes, not portraits: Karens, streamers, robotaxis, a news crew and a pigeon. Some are on your side, and soaking them costs you time.

Is there music?

No background music. Just the street, the splats and the shouting.
