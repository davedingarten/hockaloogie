SUPPORT

Something stuck?

We read every email.

Contact

Email info@davedingarten.com. If something went wrong, tell us your phone model, its iOS version and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot or a screen recording helps a lot.

I lost my progress

Your progress is saved on your device only. There is no account or cloud save yet, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts you from scratch, and we can't restore it.

What are Pardons?

Every game costs one Pardon. They refill over time, and if you miss a level's target one can let you play that level again. When you run out you can watch an ad for a free one, a few times a day, wait for the refill, or buy more. The Presidential Pardon makes them unlimited.

I bought the Presidential Pardon. Where is it on my new phone?

Open the game's settings and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pardon packs are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. An ad only plays when you tap a button that says so, and there are no ads in the middle of a game.

Privacy

Read what the game collects in the privacy policy. You can change your ad choices under Privacy choices in the game's settings.
