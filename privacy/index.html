PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, kept dry.

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (purchases added)

Hock-A-Loogie is made by Monkey Sandwich Studio in the Netherlands. You don't make an account and we never ask for your name or email. Here is what is collected and why.

Your progress stays on your device

Your scores, rank, Tears, upgrades, trophies and settings are saved on your phone (or, on the web, in your browser). They are never sent to us. Delete the app or clear the browser's data and they are gone; we can't restore them.

Anonymous play statistics

To learn how far players get and what is too hard, the game sends anonymous statistics to TelemetryDeck, which stores them on servers in the EU. These are game events such as a level cleared, a game's score, how long it lasted, a rank reached and whether something was bought in the game, together with the platform (iOS or web) and the app version.

Each install has a random ID that is hashed before it leaves the device, so the statistics can't be traced back to you or your device. They contain no name, email, location or advertising ID, TelemetryDeck doesn't store your IP address, and we only ever look at totals.

Ads, only when you ask for one

The game shows rewarded ads, and only when you tap a button to watch one, for example to replay a level or to double your Tears. There are no banners and no ads in the middle of a game. The ads come from Google AdMob.

To serve an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google may collect information about your device and how you interact with the ad, such as the device model, operating system, IP address and an identifier for this app. The ads are not personalised: the game doesn't ask to track you across other apps and doesn't use your device's advertising ID.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ad is shown. You can change them at any time under Privacy choices in the game's settings. To learn more, read Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

You can buy the Presidential Pardon or a pack of pardons in the game. Payment goes entirely through Apple's App Store: we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple only tells the game what was bought, so it can unlock it, and lets you restore a purchase on a new device. Read Apple's privacy policy.

What the game never collects

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone apart from the services above.

Children

Hock-A-Loogie is political satire for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data there is about you, and have it corrected or deleted. We hold nothing that identifies you, so in practice there is nothing on our side to look up. For ad data, use your choices in the game's settings or contact Google. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
